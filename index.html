Train Travel in Belgium

Train travel is the most convenient and efficient way to visit major cities and towns across Belgium. The rail network is extensive and services are frequent.

General Tips for Train Travel

  • Book tickets in advance, especially for peak travel times, to potentially get better prices.
  • Always validate your ticket (if required by the type of ticket purchased) before boarding the train or immediately upon boarding if validation machines are on board.
  • Check for rail passes (e.g., Rail Pass, Go Pass 10) if you plan on making multiple train journeys, as they can offer significant savings.
  • Keep an eye on departure boards at the station for platform information, as it can sometimes change.
  • Most trains offer first and second class; second class is perfectly comfortable for most journeys.

Schedules & Prices (Example)

The information below is for illustrative purposes only. Please consult the official Belgian Railways (NMBS/SNCB) website for the most current schedules and fares.

Brussels to Ghent: Approximately 30-40 EUR (one way, 2nd class). Trains run frequently, typically every 15-30 minutes. Journey time around 30-35 minutes.

Brussels to Bruges: Approximately 40-50 EUR (one way, 2nd class). Trains run frequently, typically every 20-30 minutes. Journey time around 55-60 minutes.

Brussels to Antwerp: Approximately 20-30 EUR (one way, 2nd class). Very frequent departures. Journey time around 45-50 minutes.

City Tourist Highlights

Brussels

  • Grand-Place: The stunning central square of Brussels, a UNESCO World Heritage site, surrounded by ornate guildhalls.
  • Atomium: A landmark building originally constructed for the 1958 Brussels World's Fair, offering panoramic views.
  • Manneken Pis: A famous bronze fountain sculpture of a peeing boy, which is often dressed in costumes.
  • Royal Palace of Brussels: The official palace of the King and Queen of the Belgians, open to visitors during the summer.
